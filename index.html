02 / KNOW YOUR PC

What is inside
a gaming PC?

You do not need to know every tech word. Start with these four parts and learn what each one does.

01 Graphics card GPU
The graphics card draws the images you see in games. It plays a big role in how your games look and run.
02 Processor CPU
The processor handles instructions and game tasks. It works with the other parts to keep your PC running.
03 Memory RAM
Memory holds data while your games and apps are open. Having enough memory helps your PC handle several tasks.
04 Storage SSD
Storage keeps your games, apps, and files. An SSD can help games and apps load faster than a hard drive.
